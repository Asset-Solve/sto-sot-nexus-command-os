# Use Case Research Skill

Research industry capability patterns, leading systems, standard processes, subprocesses, tasks, pain points, data needs, integration needs, AI opportunities, and controls.

Always include fit-to-standard analysis:

- What does SAP, Oracle, Maximo, ServiceNow, or another relevant system already support?
- What is configuration vs extension vs custom application?
- What clean-core or upgrade risk appears?
- What value justifies building outside the core ERP?

Output `MARKET_CAPABILITY_RESEARCH.md`, `FIT_TO_STANDARD_AND_EXTENSION_GAP.md`, `PAIN_POINT_AND_GAP_ANALYSIS.md`, `VALUE_CASE_AND_KPI_MODEL.md`.
